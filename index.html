Learn: The <marquee> Tag

Main Activity • Part A

📦 What is it?

The <marquee> tag makes text or images scroll across the screen.

It is a container tag — it has an opening <marquee> and a closing </marquee>.

👋 I am moving because I am inside a marquee!

🧩 Syntax

<marquee direction="right"
         behavior="alternate"
         scrollamount="10"
         bgcolor="yellow">
  <b>Welcome</b> to <u>Junior Web Studio</u>
</marquee>
<hr>   <!-- draws a line -->
Line one<br>Line two  <!-- new line -->

🎮 Power-Up Attributes

🧭 direction

Which way it moves.
left right up down

➡️ direction="right"

🏀 behavior

How it moves.
scroll slide alternate

🏀 alternate = bounce!

⚡ scrollamount

Speed. Bigger number = faster.
scrollamount="20"

🏎️ ZOOM! scrollamount="20"

🎨 bgcolor

Background colour.
bgcolor="yellow"

🎨 bgcolor="yellow"

🏀 Behaviour Showdown

Watch all 3 behaviours race at the same time!

scroll
🚗 I go across and come back again
Moves across, disappears, starts again ♾️
slide
🛑 I slide in and STOP
Slides in and stops at the edge 🛑
alternate
🏀 I bounce!
Bounces between both sides ↔️
<marquee behavior="scroll">...</marquee>
<marquee behavior="slide">...</marquee>
<marquee behavior="alternate">...</marquee>
